# Cut and register complete sprites

This workflow adapts selected processes from [spritesheet-expert](https://github.com/gvastethecreator/spritesheet-expert-skill): non-temporal variants, adaptive alpha gutters, reviewed source boxes, exclusive pixel ownership, hashed sources, body-root registration, contact/onion inspection and separate semantic review. The helpers are self-contained. This is not a claim that the upstream full production gate ran.

## Inspect before cutting

Run `prepare_layout.py --help`. Supply each source PNG, intended grid and a new inspection directory. The script finds transparent gutters rather than dividing the image into assumed equal cells. It fails if no clear separator exists. The output `layout.json` records boxes, source dimensions, grid order and SHA-256. Review every numbered `directions-boxes.png` or `reactions-boxes.png`: each must contain one complete character, with no missing lips, arms or feet and no neighbor pixels. If the proposed box is wrong, correct the source or author reviewed boxes; do not force a bad grid through.

If distinct characters overlap in horizontal extent, `isolate_strip.py --source input.png --out isolated.png` can separate five connected silhouettes without resampling or losing alpha pixels. For a 3x2 reaction sheet, add `--count 6 --columns 3`. It requires NumPy and SciPy. Inspect the isolated image and its layout; touching characters or an incorrect component count need source repair or regeneration.

The builder checks the recorded hash and size. Pass `--reference` and `--provenance` together. A four-pose pilot record also verifies the generated source, separated sheets and any replacement neutral. A full-pack record contains `referenceSha256` and `sources` with SHA-256 for every sheet key (`directions`, `reactions` and extras). When a character has more than one reference view, record the remaining relative paths and hashes in `additionalReferences`; the builder verifies those too. The builder requires the complete exact source set. This catches an accidental source mix before packing; hashes cannot detect a visually wrong identity. Every nonzero alpha source pixel must belong to exactly one box. It rejects overlap, uncovered pixels, opaque cells and substantial silhouette touching a box border. A PNG extension or RGBA conversion alone is not transparency: inspect alpha on light and dark backgrounds. Do not silently color-key, flatten a halo or patch anatomy after generation.

For small color deviations, complete the [material harmonization branch](color.md) before hashing layouts. Corrected source pixels require new layout hashes, reviewed color-report provenance and a new atlas; prior gaze evidence cannot be reused.

## Register without changing character size

`build_pack.py` transforms each complete owned source box onto a common square canvas. Its foot anchor is the alpha-weighted center of the lower-body band (82–96% of silhouette height) and body bottom. Review the measured root against visible feet; provide an explicit per-cell anchor in the layout only when the measurement is wrong. It uses alpha above 16 to measure the visible silhouette. Stray pixels above that threshold can still move the root or change scale: compare measured bounds and anchor against the visible feet. Preserve the original source, then remove only visually confirmed isolated contamination in empty space; never blanket-remove small components that could be fingers, tears, wings or droplets. Recompute layouts, source hashes, atlas and visual evidence after cleanup. The accepted neutral sets the base scale. Gaze poses retain one ratio per source sheet. With `--reaction-scale head-core`, reactions use the median central head width (3–35% of silhouette height) as their stable anatomical scale proxy, excluding outstretched hands. This can measure a handle, hood or other wrong feature on an unusual character. Use `--reaction-scale full-height` when separate reaction sheets draw the whole character at a different native height; the builder rejects ratios outside 0.75–1.55. Inspect gestures and decorative marks because they can affect the measured top. Use `--reaction-scale neutral` only if the source already has the same apparent scale. No mode certifies identity or anatomy. Do not independently fit a whole-pose bounding box into each cell.

`--gaze-scale body-width` uses the median body width of each gaze sheet. Perspective can change that measurement even when the character has not changed size. For separate five-view pitch strips with stable standing height, use `--gaze-scale neutral-height`: the central front pose sets one shared scale for its entire strip. Check that the middle cell really is frontal and that its feet and top are meaningful landmarks. Do not use this policy to stretch a genuinely crouching or foreshortened pose. Compare neighboring rows in the final target grid; neither policy certifies anatomical continuity.

`--occupancy` sets the neutral's height relative to the common cell. Keep an equal cell size and foot pivot for every pose. When a wide or tall gesture would leave less than 8px of measured transparent margin, enlarge **all** cells or lower occupancy and rebuild; the builder rejects the pose instead of shrinking the character. Review `scale`, `minimumMargin` and `outputBounds` in `build-report.json`; with head-core policy also review `sourceHeadWidth`, `outputHeadWidth` and `referenceHeadWidth`. Frontal reaction head widths should match the neutral within a few rendered pixels when that feature is meaningful; the lower-body anchor should not drift. A visually different torso or head shape still requires new source art, not a geometric fix.

The builder rejects insufficient border clearance, x root drift over 1.5px, bottom drift over 1px, duplicate gaze coordinates and duplicate IDs. With `--single-atlas`, all accepted full-character frames are packed into lossless `mascot.webp`; the manifest has no `layers` object. The sample has 37 640px cells in a 3200x5120 atlas. Source strips may be smaller or numerous. Actual generated source resolution must be measured and recorded; a larger packing canvas does not create source detail.

For flat ivory characters such as Moklo, `--color-limit N` takes a robust torso sample from each registered frame, compares RGB channels with neutral and rejects drift above `N`. The build report records all samples and the limit. The sample uses 22 RGB levels; its observed maximum is 14. Shading, highlights, hue and different body angles still need visual review. If another character has patterned or multicolor clothing, use a fitting visual/material check instead of forcing this ivory sampler to pass.

## Review separately

1. **Extraction:** inspect every reviewed box and alpha gutter. Hashes prove which source was cut; they do not prove a good cut.
2. **Registration and color:** inspect `alignment-contact.png`, `alignment-onion.png`, then browser playback on light and dark backgrounds. Feet should stay planted. Compare neighboring torso widths, head sizes, skin color, lip color and neck attachment. The whole head and body are already one drawing per frame.
3. **Direction and expression:** inspect each yaw row and pitch column. Confirm crown/chin exposure, far-eye visibility and lip perspective. Check that reaction faces and gestures match their labels. A pupil shift, mirror, duplicate or crossed gaze fails.

Keep failed candidates outside the active catalog. Rebuild evidence after any source, layout, anchor, label or scale change. Visual acceptance remains separate from mechanical checks.
